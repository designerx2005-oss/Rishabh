About me

A student with a
designer’s eye.

I’m Rishabh Chauhan, a student in Delhi who enjoys making things people can see, use, or take part in. Sometimes that’s a poster. Sometimes it’s a website. Sometimes it’s a chess tournament where the most important thing I make is a fixture list everyone can understand.

I like figuring out which details help someone understand an idea at a glance. That curiosity connects the things I enjoy, from choosing type for a poster to planning how someone finds their way around a website.

What I do

Graphic design

I design posters, banners, and certificates. I start by deciding what someone needs to notice first, then use type, spacing, and colour to guide their eye.

Web development

I build websites and browser-based tools. I enjoy the moment a layout becomes usable, when a visitor can follow a link, try an idea, or find exactly what they came for.

Learning UI/UX

I’m an aspiring UI/UX designer. I’m learning to look at a page through a visitor’s eyes. Can they read the text comfortably on a phone? Is it clear what happens when they tap a button?

Events and speaking

I organize student events and enjoy presenting ideas. Whether I’m explaining a hackathon or announcing a chess round, I try to give people the details they need to join in.

Projects

Web development

codePY

A Python editor that runs in the browser. I wanted someone to be able to open a page, type a few lines of Python, and see the result in the same place.

School innovation

Nagar Drishti

My smart-road concept pairs an in-car camera with GPS to spot potholes and report their location. It grew out of a simple question: could a regular journey help identify a road that needs repair?

Event organizing

Fallen Horizons

As lead organizer, I bring student web and game developers together for an online hackathon. My work covers the event identity and the details participants need to get building.

My hobbies

What keeps me curious.

Chess

I enjoy both sides of a chess tournament. At the board, I get to think through a move. As an organizer, I get to pair players, share results, and give everyone a reason to come back for the next round.

Poster design

I like seeing how much a design changes when I move a title, switch a typeface, or pick a different pair of colours. An event poster gives me a fun challenge. Someone should get a feel for the event and still be able to find the date at a glance.

Creative coding

I enjoy taking something I’ve drawn and making it respond on screen. A small experiment with HTML, CSS, or JavaScript can start with a single button and turn into a whole afternoon of figuring out how the interaction should feel.
